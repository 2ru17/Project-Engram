## Overview

This mock midterm exam covers algorithm analysis, solving recurrence relations using the Iterative Method, asymptotic bounds (Big Oh and Omega proofs), stack and queue operations, and sorting algorithms including Selection Sort, Insertion Sort, and Merge Sort.

## Important Formulas

**Recurrence Relation Example:** $T(n) = 2T(n/2) + 1$, yielding a specific closed form and Big-Oh complexity.
**Big Oh Definition:** $T(n) = O(f(n))$ if there exist constants $c$ and $n_0$ such that $T(n) \le c \cdot f(n)$ for all $n \ge n_0$.

## Worked Examples

1. **Algorithm Analysis:** Computing the total frequency count and Big-Oh for nested loops.
2. **Queue Reversal:** Reversing a queue's contents using stack operations.
3. **Sorting Iterations:** Showing the state of an array after the 5th iteration of Selection Sort or 6th iteration of Insertion Sort.
