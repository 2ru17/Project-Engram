## Theoretical Framework

The framework for Linear Algebra_Handout 11_fin relies heavily on understanding vector spaces, matrices, and their transformations. Linear equations can be represented as $ Ax = b $, and finding the determinant or employing Gaussian elimination allows us to understand the properties of these structures.

## Important Formulas

Matrix Multiplication: $ C_{ij} = \sum_{k=1}^n A_{ik} B_{kj} $
Determinant of a 2x2 matrix: $ \det(A) = ad - bc $
Inverse of a 2x2 matrix: $ A^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det(A)} \begin{pmatrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{pmatrix} $

## Worked Examples

**Example 1: Computing a Determinant**
Find the determinant of:
$$ A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix} $$
Solution:
$ \det(A) = (2)(4) - (1)(3) = 8 - 3 = 5 $

**Example 2: Matrix Addition**
Let $ A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix} $ and $ B = \begin{pmatrix} 5 & 6 \\ 7 & 8 \end{pmatrix} $.
Solution:
$ A + B = \begin{pmatrix} 6 & 8 \\ 10 & 12 \end{pmatrix} $
